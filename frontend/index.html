Petivolution: Artificial Life Evolution Simulator

Petivolution is a sophisticated ecological simulation sandbox built with Phaser 3 and React. Experience emergent biological behaviors as artificial life forms interact in a dynamic ecosystem.

Core Features

  • Utility AI Decision Making: Animals evaluate needs like hunger, thirst, and fatigue to make realistic decisions.
  • Dynamic Predator-Prey Dynamics: Observe cats hunting rats and the resulting population shifts.
  • Environmental Intervention: Place resources like water, bushes, and trash piles to influence the survival of species.
  • Browser-Based Simulation: High-performance simulation running in a Web Worker to ensure smooth visuals.

How It Works

Using agent-based modeling and a custom Utility AI system, each entity in Petivolution lives its own "life" based on a set of survival rules. Adjust the world rules to see how different environments affect the evolution of the ecosystem.
